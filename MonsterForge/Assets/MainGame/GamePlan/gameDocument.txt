I woke up to the sounds of metal hitting metal. The first sensation I felt was pure heat. I was in a room made entirely of stone. 
A man in a large leather apron and brown pants stood near the heat source. 
He was forging metal, I realized. A sword. The man glanced at me, brandishing a small dagger.
 	"You’re awake. Good. Take this." As he slides the weapon across the ground to me.
The dagger glided towards me, I grasped the hilt and turned it in my hand. The blacksmith must have seen my puzzled expression.
 	 “Name’s Burow. Found you unconscious [somewhere]. There are monsters outside the door, go deal with them.”
I hurriedly made my way towards the door. I see the face of monsters appearing.
Faces looking grotesque.
Eyes, gleaming in the dark, lit up with hateful stares.
I slash my way into them, killing all.  Took me half an hour. I went back, the door was ajar, almost like the door knew I was coming back.
The blacksmith was waiting.
Wordlessly, he hands me the sword. Then he nods.
 “You’re ready.
Ready to get rid of the filth that plagues this world.
Good luck, oh Chosen one.”

END OF DEMO.
